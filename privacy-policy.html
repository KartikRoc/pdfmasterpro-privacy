Section 1

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PDF Master Pro – Doc Scanner (the “App”), developed by KartikRoc (Kartik Gupta, India) (“we”, “us”, “our”), handles information when you use the App on Android devices.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Section 2

Information we collect

a) Documents, scans and files

When you scan a document, import an image or open a PDF, the App processes these files locally on your device to create, view, edit, merge, split, compress or convert PDFs. We do not upload, store or read your documents on our servers.

b) Camera images

If you use the scanner, the App uses your device camera to capture pages. Captured images are saved only on your device, in the location you choose.

c) Google account information (when Google Sign-In is available)

In a future version, the App will offer optional sign-in with Google. If you choose to sign in, we receive from Google:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your profile photo (if available)
  • A unique user ID created by Google / Firebase Authentication

We never receive your Google password. You can keep using the core features of the App without signing in.

d) Technical information

Google Play and Google Play services may collect basic device and app information (such as device model, Android version and crash reports) under Google’s own privacy policy.

Section 3

How we use information

  • To provide the App’s features: scanning, viewing and editing PDF files.
  • To sign you in and identify your account, if you use Google Sign-In.
  • To keep your account settings and preferences linked to you.
  • To fix bugs, improve performance and keep the App secure.
  • To reply to you when you contact us for support.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Section 4

App permissions

CAMERATo scan documents when you tap the scan button.
FILE PICKERTo open and save only the files you select. No broad storage access is requested.
INTERNETFor Google Sign-In and Google Play services. Your documents are not uploaded.

You can turn permissions off at any time in Settings → Apps → PDF Master Pro → Permissions. Some features may stop working without them.

Section 5

Third-party services

The App uses the following services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play services
  • Firebase Authentication / Google Sign-In (when sign-in is available)

If we add more services in the future, such as analytics or advertising, we will update this policy before they are used.

Section 6

Data storage and security

Your documents stay on your device. Account information from Google Sign-In is stored securely by Google Firebase, and data sent between the App and Google is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Section 7

Data retention and deletion

  • Files and scans: stay on your device until you delete them. Uninstalling the App removes its private app data.
  • Account data: kept while your account is active. You can sign out at any time.
🗑️ Delete your account: email us at the address below with the subject “Delete my account” from the email you signed in with. We will delete your account and related data within 30 days.
Section 8

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Section 9

Under applicable laws, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw your consent. To make a request, contact us using the details below.
